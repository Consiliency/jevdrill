   package and companion identities explicit. Verify the timing change offline
   through the installed reporting/replay path, then prepare the integration
   configuration and upgrade/rollback instructions. Keep background experiments
   and policy promotion disabled. Any further live run needs an allowance that
   does not reuse an exhausted ledger.
2. **Recover and reconcile the acceptance ledger.** Locate the original HOST,
   LEARN and EXPERIMENT reports from their recorded execution locations. Retain
   every historical failure. Compare source digests and phase-plan roadmap pins;
   produce an acceptance checklist tying each unchanged EC to current evidence,
   a code gap or a stated operational dependency. Refresh the candidate's
   CONTRACT and ROUTE checks first. Do not silently mark stale reports current.
3. **Complete HOST, then LEARN.** Use installed software and production
   construction paths. Supply real capability/identity callbacks; requested
   whole-run token or spending ceilings must reject executors that cannot enforce
   them. Restore/recollect native evidence and then run the respective reducers.
4. **Complete EXPERIMENT through a frozen study.** First verify execution,
   grading and recovery offline. Next prepare a representative development
   calibration protocol and resource/cost allowance for owner review. Use the
   accepted rule of no additional critical failures, with other quality tolerances
   calibrated separately for implementation, review and documentation. Retain raw
   measurements for later objective reclassification. Freeze thresholds before
   exposing fresh policy evaluation data. Candidate ordering is the only approved
   policy edit; promotion remains separately authorized.
5. **Plan and execute PREPARE after the upstream gates.** Freeze release
   thresholds before opening the untouched release holdout. Verify installation,
   upgrade, recovery and rollback on supported native platforms. Produce the
   release manifest and review bundle before requesting publication or adoption.
